Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Factnory ("Factnory", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and our use of third-party platform APIs (including the TikTok API) to publish content ("the Service").

1. Information We Collect

Our website does not use tracking cookies or collect personal information from visitors. When we connect our own Factnory account to a platform such as TikTok, YouTube, or Facebook in order to publish content, the platform's API may provide us with basic account information (such as an account identifier and display name) solely to allow us to publish and manage our own posts on that account.

2. How We Use Information

Any account information obtained through a platform's API is used exclusively to authenticate our own account and to publish, schedule, and check the status of the content we upload. We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and related credentials are stored securely and are used only by our internal automation systems to operate the Service. We take reasonable measures to protect this information from unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Platforms

When our content is viewed or shared on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, or any other third-party platform, your interaction with that content is subject to the respective platform's own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

5. Data Retention

We retain platform account information only for as long as necessary to operate the Service, and we delete or revoke access tokens if we no longer need them or if access is revoked by the account holder or the platform.

6. Your Rights

If you have questions about the information we hold or would like it removed, you can contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
