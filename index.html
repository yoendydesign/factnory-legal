How Factnory publishes

We produce our videos with an internal workflow and publish them to our own social accounts. On TikTok, we use the official TikTok API:

  1. We sign in to our Factnory TikTok account with Login Kit (TikTok's official authorization).
  2. Our system uploads each finished video using the Content Posting API.
  3. The video is posted to our Factnory profile with its caption. We only publish to accounts we own and have authorized.
